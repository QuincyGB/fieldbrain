# Northgate Depot — Maintenance Log Excerpts, 2026

*Fictional sample log written for the FieldBrain demo.*

Site: Northgate Depot (light industrial). Equipment on site: one AeroCool RTU-9000
rooftop unit serving the office block, one Meridian AH-40 air handler serving the
warehouse offices.

## 2026-01-19 — RTU-9000, filter change
Replaced return-air filters (90-day cycle). Belt inspected, deflection within spec.
No faults logged since the last visit.

## 2026-03-14 — RTU-9000, fault E47 (compressor short-cycling)
Callout: office staff reported the unit "starting and stopping every couple of
minutes," display showing E47. Found the condenser coil heavily matted with lint
from the workshop extraction vent nearby. Washed the coil inside-out, restored
power, and observed three full cooling cycles with normal run times. Fault did not
recur. Recommendation recorded on the work order: wash the condenser coil every
spring for this site because of the lint load, instead of the standard schedule.
Closed: resolved on site, no parts used.

## 2026-04-02 — AH-40, zone complaint
Warehouse office zone too warm. Found VAV damper actuator coupling loose on the
shaft (matches fault F03 pattern in the AH-40 notes). Tightened the set screw,
stroked the damper twice, zone recovered within the hour. No parts used.

## 2026-05-27 — RTU-9000, pre-season service
Filters replaced, condensate drain trap cleared and flushed, coil inspected — light
dust only, washed as preventive. Thermostat cycle-rate confirmed at 3 cycles per
hour. Suction and discharge pressures recorded normal at a 29 C ambient.

## 2026-07-08 — AH-40, valve actuator
Chilled-water valve actuator failed (fault F14); valve stuck at 40 percent. Actuator
replaced from van stock, valve stroked and modulation verified. Zone temperatures
normal the following day.
